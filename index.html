About Avenrix

Building Critical Healthcare Environments with Precision, Expertise & Trust

Avenrix Healthcare Solutions is a turnkey healthcare and pharmaceutical infrastructure company focused on designing, supplying, and executing solutions for critical and controlled environments.

We bring together technical expertise, quality-driven products, and end-to-end project execution to create healthcare and pharmaceutical spaces that meet the demanding requirements of safety, hygiene, precision, and operational efficiency.

Our expertise spans a comprehensive range of solutions, including AHUs & HVAC Systems, Modular Operation Theatres, Cleanrooms, IVF Laboratories, Medical Gas Pipeline Systems, Wall & Ceiling Panels, Specialized Flooring, and Medical Equipment.
